## Maintenance Photo & Description Triage Rules
1. Classify the maintenance issue into one of: `plumbing`, `electrical`, `hvac`, `appliance`, `structural`, `pest`, `locks_keys`, or `general`.
2. Assign urgency: `routine`, `urgent`, or `emergency`.
3. Ask at most two short follow-up questions if critical diagnostic detail is missing (for example, shutoff valve accessibility or appliance model number).
4. Produce a staff-reviewable draft summary only. Nothing is created in PropertyDek until staff or resident confirmation completes.
